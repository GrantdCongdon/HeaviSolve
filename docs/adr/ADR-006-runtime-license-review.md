# ADR-006: exact runtime-license exceptions for the clean FEM research environment

Status: accepted declared-license review for the 13 pinned packages below; **not a binary-release certification**. Date: 2026-11-03.

## Context and decision

ADR-002 deliberately rejects unknown expressions. The smaller FEM environment in ADR-005 contains 13 packages with expressions absent from that initial list. Examination of the license files shipped with those exact conda archives, their recipes and primary upstream texts distinguishes compatible terms from the prohibited ParMETIS grant. Add narrowly scoped admission records in `tools/conda-license-reviews.toml`; do not broaden the generic allow-list or use substring matching. The original ParMETIS rejection remains unchanged.

Each record binds name, version, build, archive SHA256, platform and the original license expression. Any mismatch requires fresh review. `selected_license` records a deliberate alternative where relevant. The evidence path identifies the local package-cache license file actually read; its SHA256 permits comparison after re-fetching the pinned package. The cache itself is not committed. Sources and obligations remain human-reviewable. The conda metadata hash identifies an archive, not an independent authentication of upstream authorship.

This ADR extends the plan's initial allow-list for these particular permissive licenses and additional-permission expressions. It does not admit proprietary, noncommercial, field-of-use or GPL-2.0-only grants. Scope is the declared licenses of a research environment. Before distribution, audit linked/bundled files, patches and provider modules, assemble notices and corresponding source, and verify required relinking rights. Passing the metadata scanner cannot establish those obligations were fulfilled.

## Compatibility reasoning and limits

These are project licensing decisions based on the grants, not legal certification. Permissive notice/nonendorsement obligations do not impose the denied field-of-use restrictions. The extra permissions in LLVM/GCC expressions do not weaken HeaviSolve's AGPL license. The FSF lists LGPL2.1 as GPLv3-compatible and expressly extends its compatibility matrix to AGPLv3. [FSF license list](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/license-list.html#LGPLv2.1), [FSF compatibility FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#AllCompatibility).

Unknown versions, build hashes, license expressions and blank metadata still fail closed. The earlier rejected broad environment also includes CeCILL-C/unknown packages and remains rejected. No new exception approves ParMETIS. Binary artifacts are not shipped by this ADR. R4 release legal review, complete source offers and redistribution packaging remain required.
