# S4: meshing feasibility

**LOCAL PASS for the ring mesh quality criterion; hosted CI pending.** Two native C++ paths now produce positive-Jacobian ring meshes. This is research evidence, not a verified optical solver or a general process extrusion engine.

Gmsh 4.15.2 creates a radius 10/9 annulus, extrudes 0.5 (dimensionless test units), then tetrahedralizes at size 0.35–0.5. The gate declared in source is positive minimum Jacobian and minSICN > 0.05. Initial mesh: 2617 elements, minSICN 0.020583581134155957 — FAIL. Applying `mesh::optimize("Netgen")` yields 4324 elements, minSICN 0.42782904872064897 and minimum Jacobian 0.0082792378589275177 — PASS. Both outputs remain stored. This exercises Netgen optimization through Gmsh, not an independent Netgen adapter.

The custom polar extrusion prototype uses 128 annular sectors, 512 vertices and 256 prisms. It serializes and reimports an MFEM mesh, preserving 768 boundary elements, then assembles a first-order H(curl) mass matrix with 1280 DOFs. Its predeclared gates require positive Jacobians, minimum scaled Jacobian > 0.05, relative volume error against the polygonal annulus < 1e-12, and against the circular annulus < 1e-3. [Measured output](../../verification/results/s4-extrusion.log): volume 29.833145991069618 versus polygon volume 29.833145991070154; circular relative error 0.00040154685033877424; minimum Jacobian 0.22080453447337967; minimum scaled Jacobian 0.44481833712857255. All pass. The scaled-Jacobian quantity is determinant divided by the product of Jacobian-column norms; it is not Gmsh's SICN metric.

Build `verification/meshing/ring.cpp` with C++20 and Gmsh headers/library. Build `verification/meshing/extrusion.cpp` with C++20 and the MFEM 4.8 serial headers/static library used by the Poisson spike, linking its LAPACK/BLAS dependencies. No external geometry data is bundled. [Gmsh license](https://gmsh.info/LICENSE.txt) is GPL-2.0-or-later with a named-library combination exception, not a general proprietary-linking exception. The GPLv3 option fits ADR-002. Existing host binaries are research infrastructure; redistribution requires transitive audit.

Recommendation for Phase R integration review: Gmsh for general tetrahedral meshing, custom extrusion for layered prism geometry, with MFEM as the mesh consumer. Adoption remains conditional on the component ADR and audited reproducible builds. Unresolved coverage includes multiple materials/interfaces, general layout polygons, independent aspect-ratio and wavelength/skin-depth gates, standalone Netgen comparison, and Linux/macOS CI. [Evidence provenance](../../verification/results/s4-s6-s7-provenance.json) records capture-time source and raw-output hashes.
